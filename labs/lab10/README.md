# Lab 10: Change control and scope triage

**Chapters:** Chapter 11 (Assignment 4) of *Information Technology Project Management: Traditional Foundations, Agile Delivery and AI-Enhanced Practice*  
**Files:** `docs/change_request_CR-014.docx`

## Purpose
Run integrated change control on a real request.

## You will produce

1. A formal impact assessment of CR-014 for the change control board, covering every dimension in Section 11.5 (**Assignment 4**).
2. A recommendation with conditions.
3. An updated change log and burn-up chart showing the effect of the decision.

## The document

`docs/change_request_CR-014.docx` is the change request as triaged: the request, its reasons and urgency, the team's estimates and the concerns raised by the Medical Director and the Data Protection Officer. The impact assessment, options, recommendation and decision are for you to complete.

## Steps

1. Read the request and the case facts it points to.
2. Assess the options (reject, defer, the limited version and the full version) on scope, schedule, cost, quality and safety, compliance, risk and benefits, with figures. Appendix C, Table C.13 is a checklist.
3. Recommend a decision the board can take, with conditions that are specific and have owners.
4. Update a change log (Appendix C template) and redraw the burn-up to show the effect.

## Tips

- "It fits in the headroom" is not enough. Ask what else that headroom will be needed for.
- Operating costs and compliance work count, even when they are not story points.
