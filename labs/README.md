# Labs

Twelve labs practise the methods of Chapters 1 to 12 on the HealthSync case. Each chapter's Lab link box says which lab goes with it.

| Lab | Topic | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| [1](lab01/) | Navigating the modern IT tool stack; Scrum and Kanban boards | Chapters 1 and 2 |
| [2](lab02/) | Initiating the project and stakeholder mapping | Chapter 3 |
| [3](lab03/) | Requirements architecture and backlog refinement | Chapter 4 |
| [4](lab04/) | Agile estimation and release planning | Chapter 5 |
| [5](lab05/) | Scheduling, critical path and PERT | Chapter 5 |
| [6](lab06/) | Cloud economics and FinOps budgeting | Chapter 6 |
| [7](lab07/) | Continuous integration and automated quality gates | Chapter 8 |
| [8](lab08/) | Quantifying IT risk and building a digital register | Chapter 7 |
| [9](lab09/) | Tracking performance via dashboards | Chapter 11 |
| [10](lab10/) | Change control and scope triage | Chapter 11 (Assignment 4) |
| [11](lab11/) | Deployment, cutover planning and rolling out | Chapter 12 |
| [12](lab12/) | Agile retrospectives and project offboarding | Chapter 12 |
| [AI](ai_practice/) | Prompt patterns, test sets and AI governance | 13 and Appendix A |

The case facts are in `case/` and the data files in `data/`. Your tutor will tell you which labs are assessed and when they are due.
