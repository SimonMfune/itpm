# Lab 4: Agile estimation and release planning

**Chapters:** Chapter 5 of *Information Technology Project Management: Traditional Foundations, Agile Delivery and AI-Enhanced Practice*  
**Files:** `data/healthsync_backlog.csv`

## Purpose
Estimate HealthSync stories as a team and forecast the release.

## You will produce

1. A Planning Poker session on twenty HealthSync stories, with your estimates and the reference stories you used.
2. A release burn-up chart for the 640-point MVP at three velocities.

## Steps

1. Take twenty stories with no points from `data/healthsync_backlog.csv`. Agree two or three reference stories from those that do have points.
2. Run Planning Poker: everyone reveals at once, the highest and lowest explain, then re-estimate. Record the first and final estimates, and note where the team disagreed most and why.
3. Build a release burn-up in a spreadsheet: a scope line at 640 points and completed-work lines at 74, 68 and 60 points a sprint (Figure 5.5). Mark the sprint in which each line crosses the scope.
4. Write three sentences for the steering group: the likely release sprint, the range and what would change it.

## Tips

- Estimate relative size, not hours. If the team starts converting points to hours, stop and compare with a reference story instead.
- A single date is not a forecast. Give a range.
