# Complication cards

Your tutor releases complications during the semester, as HealthSync's three complication cards were released at set weeks. A card describes something that happens to your project: a new demand from a stakeholder, a supplier failure, a person leaving. How your team responds will show more than any plan.

The cards are not published here, so that nobody sees one before its week. When your tutor gives you a card:

1. Log it as an issue or change request, with the date you received it.
2. Assess its impact on scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk and compliance.
3. Decide your response as a team, record the decision and its owner in your decision log, and update your baselines.
4. Explain the complication and your response in your post-mortem.
