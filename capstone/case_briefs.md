# Capstone case briefs

*These cases are fictional.* Each can replace HealthSync for the capstone. Your tutor will release complications during the semester, as with HealthSync's three complication cards.

**1. CareGrid laboratory information system.** CareGrid's second-ranked project from Chapter 3. Replace paper and spreadsheet records in the laboratories at Central Hospital and the seven clinics with a system that receives test orders from HealthSync, tracks samples, records results from analysers and returns results to clinicians. Constraints: twelve months; capital K4.2 million; patient data in Zambia; integration with HealthSync's records; laboratory accreditation requirements for audit trails. Likely complications: an analyser supplier refuses to share its interface; the laboratory manager resigns; the business case must be full-cost.

**2. Lusaka municipal business licensing portal.** A city council wants businesses to apply for, pay for and renew trading licences online, replacing counter queues and paper files. Constraints: nine months; K3.5 million; mobile money and card payments; integration with the council's revenue system; accessibility for users with basic phones; compliance with the Electronic Government Act. Likely complications: a change of council leadership; a payment provider outage; staff resistance at the counters.

**3. Kalulu Agri-Credit loan scoring (Chapter 13).** Develop the AI-assisted loan scoring system from Chapter 13's mini case as a gated ML project, with an interim AI usage policy and a supplier decision. Constraints: six months to shadow mode; K2.8 million; personal data processed in Zambia; performance targets by group, including women farmers. Likely complications: biased historical data; a vendor offering a ready-made model; loan officers fearing replacement.
