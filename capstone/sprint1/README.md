# Capstone sprint I: setting up the team

**Chapter:** 9 of *Information Technology Project Management: Traditional Foundations, Agile Delivery and AI-Enhanced Practice*

## You will produce

1. A team charter with working agreements: core hours, response times, how decisions are made and how disagreements are handled (`../templates/team_charter.md`).
2. A RACI chart for your capstone deliverables (`raci.csv`).
3. A skills matrix, with responses to any single points of knowledge (`skills_matrix.csv`).
4. A communication plan for your tutor and any client.
5. A first team health check, repeated at each capstone sprint (`health_check.csv`).
6. At the end of the first sprint, a retrospective that includes an anonymous safety check.

## Tips

- One person Accountable for each deliverable. If two people are accountable, nobody is.
- A skill only one person has is a risk. Plan pairing now, not when that person is ill.
- Health check results belong to the team. Discuss trends; do not use them to judge individuals.
