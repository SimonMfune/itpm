# Project document templates (Appendix C)

Editable versions of the templates in Appendix C of *Information Technology Project Management: Traditional Foundations, Agile Delivery and AI-Enhanced Practice*.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `ITPM_Templates_Narrative.docx` | All eleven templates of Appendix C, Sections C.1 to C.11: business case, charter, stakeholder register, WBS dictionary, user stories with Definitions of Ready and Done, risk register, RACI and team charter, change request, status report, go/no-go checklist, lessons register and closure report |
| `ITPM_Templates_Registers.xlsx` | Stakeholder register, WBS dictionary, risk register, change log, earned value, RACI and lessons register, with the Kafubu Home Supplies case (Appendix C, Section C.12) as a worked example |

Replace the example rows with your own project's data. Formulas are in the grey columns.
